# Third-party services and content

- Quran text and related Quranic content: Quranpedia API, https://api.quranpedia.net/ . The application queries live endpoints and does not redistribute a data dump. Usage policy: https://quranpedia.net/api-docs#usage-policy
- Hadith search and grading: Dorar Hadith Encyclopedia API, https://dorar.net/article/389 . Follow Dorar's API terms and seek permission before extensive use.
- OCR runtime: Tesseract.js loaded from jsDelivr (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
- Arabic typeface: IBM Plex Sans Arabic loaded from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License): https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+Arabic

The MIT license in this repository applies only to original project code, not third-party services or their content.
